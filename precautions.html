Precautions

General awareness based on public guidance from CDC, NIDDK and WHO. It is not personal medical advice.

🥗 Eat well

  • Choose vegetables, fruit, whole grains and pulses.
  • Limit sugary drinks, sweets and highly processed food.
  • Watch portion sizes.

🏃 Stay active

  • Adults are generally advised about 150 minutes of moderate activity a week.
  • Reduce long hours of sitting.

⚖️ Healthy weight

  • Even modest weight loss helps people who are overweight lower their Type 2 risk.

🚭 Avoid tobacco

  • Smoking raises the risk of Type 2 diabetes and of complications.

🩺 Regular check-ups

  • Check blood pressure and blood sugar as your doctor advises, especially with family history or overweight.

💊 Never self-medicate

  • Do not start, stop or change medicines or insulin without a doctor.

👣 If you have diabetes

  • Check your feet daily for cuts and sores.
  • Follow your treatment plan and attend eye and kidney check-ups.

⚠️ See a doctor soon if you notice

  • Constant thirst and frequent urination
  • Unexplained weight loss or constant tiredness
  • Blurred vision or wounds that heal slowly

Emergency: confusion, fainting, fruity-smelling breath, vomiting or trouble breathing need urgent medical care.
