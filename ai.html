AI Behind This Project

Honest summary: this is a simple rule-based classifier, not deep learning. The rules were written by a student from commonly known risk factors, not learned from patient data, and are not clinically validated.

Example

Input: age 60+, low activity, family history (parent), BMI 28.
Processing: 3 + 2 + 3 + 3 = 11 points.
Output: Higher Risk category (educational, not a diagnosis).

AI concepts shown

Data collected

8 answers typed by the user. Nothing is saved or sent.

Features

Age group, BMI, activity, family history, blood pressure, smoking, diet.

Preprocessing

Range checks, converting height and weight into BMI, converting choices to numbers.

Classification

The score is mapped to one of three classes using thresholds.

Responsible AI

Privacy

No account, no identifying questions, no database. The calculation runs in your browser.

Bias and fairness

BMI cut-offs and risk differ between populations, so a simple rule may be unfair to some people, such as athletes.

False positives

Says higher risk when the person is actually fine. Causes worry and extra tests.

False negatives

Says lower risk when the person actually has a problem. This may delay care.

Limitations

Only 7 features, no blood tests, not validated, self-reported data may be wrong.

Human oversight

AI can support awareness, but only a doctor can diagnose. A prediction is never a diagnosis, and humans must make final health decisions.
